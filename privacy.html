Atlas by Auxin Studio privacy

Atlas runs locally in your Chrome browser. Collected records and search preferences remain in Chrome extension storage on your device; the extension does not send them to a developer server or third-party analytics service.

Records remain stored until you export them or choose “Reset data.” Export files are generated locally in the Atlas side panel.

The interface language selector uses prewritten translations bundled with the extension. Language changes are applied locally; interface text is not sent to a translation provider. The extension uses activeTab, scripting, storage, unlimitedStorage, and sidePanel. It requests optional access to https://www.google.com/* only when you choose to open or scan a Google Maps search, so the persistent side panel can continue working after navigation to Maps.

Atlas reads business-listing text rendered in the current Google Maps page and may inspect publicly displayed business profile fields when you request phone lookup. It does not bypass login, CAPTCHA, rate limits, or access controls. Use it only in ways permitted by applicable laws and website terms.

For worldwide filename normalization, Atlas downloads the public GeoNames cities500 dataset, with a fallback dataset from joelacus/world-cities. Only public place names and country codes are downloaded and cached locally in Chrome storage; Atlas does not send your search terms, collected business records, or exports to either host. The dataset is available under CC BY 4.0 and is provided as-is.
